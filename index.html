Background

I am currently completing my Master 2 in Integration, Circuits and Systems (ICS) at Institut Polytechnique de Paris and will be joining Université Paris-Saclay as a PhD student in October 2026, specializing in analog integrated circuit design and microelectronics.

My background bridges experimental physics and microelectronics: during five years (2017–2022) as a Research Associate at Sorbonne Paris Nord University (Laser Physics Laboratory / LSPM), I investigated laser physics, photopolymerization kinetics, optical instrumentation, and direct laser writing of organic-inorganic hybrid nanomaterials. Building on this strong foundation in physical device principles and experimental methodologies, I transitioned into integrated circuit design and microelectronic systems.

My papers and conference presentations are on my publications page, and my CV is available here.
